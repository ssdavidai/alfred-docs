title: "Decisions"
description: "The audit ledger of every choice Alfred has made on the principal's behalf."

`/desk` shows the **outstanding** decisions. `/decisions` shows the **ledger** — every choice Alfred has made on the principal's behalf, every signal the steward acted on, every state change cascaded through the system, sorted newest-first with a 30-day stacked-bar chart at the top *(packages/web/src/dashboard/DecisionsPage.tsx:1-13)*.

## Two tabs

The page is split into two top-level tabs *(DecisionsPage.tsx:28-30, 304-342)*:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Decisions">
    The HANDLED / HELD / ASKED ledger plus the 30-day chart. Source is the merge of three queries — `getRecentJudgments`, `getStewardFeed` (event/steward-action records from the background workflow), and `getRecentDecisions` (the `decision/*` records written by every Desk click) *(DecisionsPage.tsx:128-148)*.

    Each row is normalised into `{ when, input, conf, outcome, state, intent, matterRef, taskRef, sourceSignal }` so the three sources land in the same table.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="State Changes">
    The cross-target `state_change` feed. Reads `getStateChanges` and `getStateChangeSources`. This is the source-of-truth audit for every mutation that flowed through the state-mutator — matter status flips, task transitions, narrative refreshes, briefing-driven changes.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## The 30-day stacked-bar chart

A hand-drawn inline SVG (no recharts dependency — the chart is intentionally austere). Each bar represents one of the last 30 days; the bar is stacked in three segments — **handled**, **held**, **asked** *(DecisionsPage.tsx:248-291)*.

- *Handled* = decisions with `intent: done` or `intent: take_mine` (the principal closed the loop, themselves or via Alfred)
- *Held* = `intent: defer` (decision parked, no agent fired)
- *Asked* = `intent: delegate` (an agent was asked to do the work)

Hovering a bar shows the per-outcome counts; the totals row sums them across the window. The y-axis is auto-scaled to the busiest day in the window.

## The filter pills

Four toggles below the chart — **All** (default), **Handled**, **Held**, **Asked** *(DecisionsPage.tsx:26-27, 149)*. They filter the rows below the chart without rebucketing the chart itself.

## The decision intents

A Desk click can carry one of five intents *(DecisionsPage.tsx:215-221, packages/web/src/dashboard/DeskPage.tsx:73-78)*:

| Intent | Outcome pill | What ctrl-api does |
|---|---|---|
| **Done** | Handled | Flip the source needs-attention card to handled, stamp `synchronous_flip: true`, write `decision/<ts>.md` with `state: open`. `DecisionRouterWorkflow` skips the action path; `extract_observation_from_decision` still fires. |
| **Defer** | Held | Park the card with an optional `execute_at`; the scheduled-decisions worker re-surfaces it at that time. |
| **Noise** | Handled | One-tap gesture — materialises a `signal_noise_pattern` instinct so future events of the same kind are suppressed at the router. No note, no panel. |
| **Delegate** | Asked | Dispatch the action to an agent. `DecisionRouterWorkflow` calls `dispatch_action_to_agent` directly with three idempotency layers (state guard, `dispatching` mark, `side_effects.agent_dispatched`). |
| **Take mine** | Handled | Spawn a `to_do/<ts>.md` so the item lands in the principal's persistent Backstage tray on `/desk`. |

Each row also carries a **state badge** showing the decision's lifecycle position — `open`, `scheduled`, `executing`, `completed`, or `reversed` *(DecisionsPage.tsx:33-38)* — so the principal can scan what's settled vs what's in flight.

## Provenance — what the row came from

Each row carries up to three provenance pills *(DecisionsPage.tsx:51-75, 234-237)*:

- **Matter** — derived from the decision's `matter_ref` (stem only — `[[matter/foo]]` → `foo`)
- **Task** — same treatment for `task_ref`
- **Source signal** — the originating `signal/...` path or `source_record` ULID, shown as a short label

These pills link back to the live record so the principal can trace any audit row to the signal that started it.

<Note>
The ledger polls `getRecentDecisions` every 15 seconds *(DecisionsPage.tsx:140-147)* so a freshly-clicked Desk decision lands on `/decisions` within a quarter-minute. The judgments and steward queries refresh every 60 seconds because their writers are slower-cadence.
</Note>

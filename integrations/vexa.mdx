<Warning>
**Vexa is retired** as of PR #126 (2026-05-29). Its replacement is
[Recall.ai](/integrations/recall) — a hosted SaaS meeting bot that fans
out per-meeting bots to Zoom / Google Meet / Microsoft Teams / Cisco Webex,
streams transcripts back via webhook, **and** lets Alfred speak inside
the meeting via Recall's Output Media webpage feature.
</Warning>

This page is preserved as a historical record of what got removed.

## Why Vexa was retired

The 0522 fleet audit found `profiles: ["vexa"]` had not been up on a
single live tenant since the single-VM cutover — `docker ps -a` returned
zero `vexa-*` matches fleet-wide. Three structural bugs:

1. `POST /api/v1/admin/vexa/auto-join` read `/srv/alfred-black/.env` —
   a host path with no mount inside the ctrl-api container → hard 500
   → "Internal ctrl-api error" in the dashboard, ~9 months post-cutover.
2. `register_schedules.py` create-or-deletes the two Temporal schedules
   on `VEXA_ENABLED`; `routes/vexa.ts` tried to pause/unpause them. On
   disable the schedules were *gone* — unpause-on-enable `NOT_FOUND`'d.
3. There was no historical transcript corpus on any tenant — nothing
   to migrate.

## What got removed

- **Nine services**: `vexa-redis`, `vexa-postgres`, `vexa-minio`,
  `vexa-minio-init`, `vexa-admin-api`, `vexa-runtime-api`,
  `vexa-meeting-api`, `vexa-api-gateway`, `vexa-dashboard`.
- **Four named volumes**: `vexa_redis`, `vexa_postgres`, `vexa_minio`,
  `vexa_recordings`.
- **~244 LoC** from `docker-compose.yaml`.
- **Two ctrl-api routes** stubbed to `410 Gone`: `routes/vexa.ts`,
  `routes/webhooks/vexa.ts`.
- **Two Wasp actions** removed: `getVexaAutoJoin`, `setVexaAutoJoin`.
- The Vexa `/channels` card flipped to a placeholder, then re-skinned
  as the Recall card.
- Four template files (`vexa-stack.yaml.njk`, `vexa-profiles.yaml`,
  …) and one Caddy matcher entry.
- 10 environment variables (`VEXA_*`).

A roughly ~3 GB resident memory ceiling and a docker-socket-mounting
`runtime-api` container disappeared with the migration.

## Where to go now

See **[Recall.ai](/integrations/recall)** for the replacement integration —
same problem space (auto-join meetings, transcribe, fan actions into
signals), different shape (hosted SaaS, configured entirely from
`/channels`, no extra containers, plus the new in-meeting voice surface).
